# AI Voice Agent — Real Estate Lead Qualifier & Booking Assistant

## What it does
Answers inbound calls, qualifies the buyer or renter, and books a property viewing — a technical demonstration of automated call handling and lead qualification for real estate agencies.

## Tech stack
- Retell AI (single-prompt voice agent)
- LLM-driven conversation logic
- Browser-based test calling

## How it works
```
Inbound call → qualification questions (buy/rent, area, budget, bedrooms, timeline) → viewing booked → details confirmed
```

## What it handles on a call
- Buy or rent, area, budget, bedrooms, move-in timeline
- Financing / pre-approval status for buyers
- Books a viewing — day and time
- Collects name and number, confirms everything back

## Guardrails
- Never invents listings, prices, or availability
- No legal, tax, or mortgage advice
- Escalates to a human agent for anything outside its scope
